## Collection of Your Information

We collect information about you in various ways, including:

### Personal Data

Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, or phone number, when you register, engage in Site activities like chat or message boards, or interact with our services. Providing this information is optional, but refusal may limit access to certain features.

### Derivative Data

Data automatically collected by our servers, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and pages viewed before and after accessing the Site.

### Financial Data

Payment-related information (e.g., credit card number, card brand, expiration date) collected during purchases or service inquiries. This data is stored by our payment processor, and we recommend reviewing their privacy policy for details.

### Mobile Device Data

Device details, such as mobile device ID, model, or manufacturer, if you access the Site from a mobile device.

### Third-Party Data

Information obtained from third parties, such as marketing leads or personal data, with your consent.

### Data From Contests, Giveaways, and Surveys

Information you provide when participating in contests, giveaways, or surveys.

## Use of Your Information

Accurate information enables us to deliver a seamless, efficient, and personalized experience. We may use your information to:

- Manage contests, promotions, or sweepstakes.
- Compile anonymous statistical data for internal or third-party analysis.
- Create and maintain your account.
- Deliver targeted ads, newsletters, or promotional updates.
- Communicate about your account or orders.
- Process purchases, payments, and refunds.
- Build a personalized profile to enhance future Site visits.
- Optimize Site performance and functionality.
- Analyze usage trends to improve user experience.
- Notify you of Site updates or new offerings.
- Prevent fraud, monitor for theft, or protect against illegal activity.
- Address disputes, troubleshoot issues, or respond to service requests.
- Send newsletters or solicit Site support.

## Tracking Technologies

### Cookies and Web Beacons

We use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to enhance and personalize your Site experience. See our Cookie Policy for details, which is incorporated into this Privacy Policy. Using the Site signifies agreement to the Cookie Policy.

### Internet-Based Advertising

We may use third-party software for email marketing or interactive campaigns, which may employ cookies or similar technologies to optimize your experience. You can opt out of interest-based ads via the Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out Tool.

### Website Analytics

We partner with third-party vendors, such as Google Analytics, to track Site usage and content popularity using first- and third-party cookies. By accessing the Site, you consent to this data collection. Review the vendors’ privacy policies for details or opt out via their settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out Tool. Changing devices or browsers may affect opt-out settings.

## Policy for Children

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 13. If we learn we have collected such data, please contact us, and we will take appropriate action.

## Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most browsers and mobile devices offer a Do-Not-Track (DNT) feature to signal your preference against online tracking. As no universal DNT standard exists, we do not currently respond to DNT signals. If a standard is adopted, we will update this policy accordingly.

## California Privacy Rights

Under California’s “Shine The Light” law (Civil Code Section 1798.83), California residents may request, once per year, free information about personal data shared with third parties for direct marketing in the prior calendar year. Submit requests using the contact information below.

California residents under 18 with registered accounts may request removal of publicly posted data. Contact us with your account email and a statement of California residency. We will ensure the data is not publicly displayed, though complete removal from our systems is not guaranteed.
