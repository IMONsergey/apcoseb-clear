We recommend reviewing this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed.

## Use of Cookies

A cookie is a small data file that assigns you a unique identifier stored on your device. When you interact with the Site, your browser uses this identifier to facilitate your requests.

Cookies enable us to:

- Track services used.
- Store registration details.
- Remember preferences.
- Maintain login sessions.
- Streamline purchases.
- Monitor page visits.

They help us analyze Site usage and improve your experience.

We use the following cookie types to optimize your experience:

- **Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Essential for the Site’s core functionality, including security, authentication, and payment processing.
- **Personalization Cookies:** Tailor your experience by saving preferences and settings.
- **Analytics Cookies:** Provide insights into Site usage to enhance our services.
- **Advertising Cookies:** Deliver relevant ads and measure their effectiveness.

## Cookies We May Use

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are vital for security, network management, and accessibility. Disabling them via browser settings may impair Site functionality.

- **Landing Page Cookies:** Store preferences for privacy settings, login, or form submissions.
- **CSRF Protection Cookie:** Safeguards your data during Site interactions.
- **Google reCAPTCHA Service Cookie:** Protects against spam and abuse.
- **Google Storage Cookie:** Supports security features like authentication and fraud prevention.
- **Payment Service Provider Cookie:** Facilitates secure transactions via our payment provider, Paddle.com.

### Personalization Cookies

These cookies recognize returning visitors and support features like language settings.

- **Google Functionality Storage Cookie:** Enhances Site functionality, such as language preferences.
- **Google Personalization Storage Cookie:** Personalizes content, like video recommendations.

### Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand Site usage to improve performance, including Google Analytics cookies.

### Advertising Cookies

These cookies deliver tailored advertisements and track ad performance, including:

- **Google Storage Cookies for Advertising:** Support ad delivery.
- **Google Consent Cookie for Advertising Data:** Manages user data sent to Google for ads.
- **Google Consent Cookie for Personalized Advertising:** Enables customized ad experiences.

## Other Tracking Technologies

We may use web beacons, pixel tags, or similar technologies to customize the Site and improve your experience.

These tools, embedded in pages or emails, track metrics like page visits or email views, collecting limited data such as cookie numbers, timestamps, and page details.

While you cannot decline web beacons or pixel tags, **controlling cookies that interact with them** can limit their functionality.
