## 1. Definitions

- **GDPR:** The General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
- **Personal Data:** Information that can identify an individual, such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, or sharing.
- **Subprocessor:** A third-party provider we engage to process Personal Data.

## 2. Scope of This Agreement

- This Agreement applies to all Personal Data you provide while using our services.
- We process your Personal Data to deliver and enhance our services, as described in our **Privacy Policy**.
- We act as the **Data Processor** for your Personal Data, while you, as the **Data Controller**, determine the purpose and means of data collection and processing.

## 3. What Data We Process

We may process the following Personal Data:

- Names, email addresses, and contact information.
- IP addresses and metadata generated from your use of our services.
- Other data you submit through your account or communications with us.

## 4. How We Protect Your Data

We prioritize your privacy and employ robust measures to safeguard your data, including:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted during transmission.
- **Access Control:** Only authorized personnel can access Personal Data.
- **Monitoring:** Continuous assessments to detect and address vulnerabilities.

## 6. Data Transfers

- Personal Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA) when necessary, with safeguards like **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** to comply with GDPR.
- For users in other regions, we adhere to local data protection laws to ensure security.

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** Obtain a copy of your Personal Data.
- **Correction:** Rectify inaccurate data.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data.
- **Restriction:** Limit the use of your data.
- **Portability:** Transfer your data to another provider.

To exercise these rights, contact **info@apcosys.net**.

## 8. Data Breaches

In the event of a data breach affecting your Personal Data, we will notify you promptly, detailing:

- The nature of the breach.
- Its potential impact.
- Mitigation measures taken.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data only as long as necessary to:

- Provide our services.
- Comply with legal obligations.

Data no longer needed is securely deleted.

We may revise this Agreement to reflect updates to our services or legal requirements. Changes will be posted here with the effective date clearly indicated.
